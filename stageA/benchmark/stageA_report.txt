FINDINGS:
1. Optimisation level made only a small difference. Using -O1 to -O3 made Stage A just 1.01–1.12x faster than -O0 (from 5.64 s down to 5.06 s at best). The averages do improve step by step from -O0 to -O3, but runs at the same level varied by about 1 s (e.g. -O0 ranged from 5.29 s to 6.33 s), which is larger than the 0.58 s total gain, so the small differences between levels are not reliable.
2. Reading the file is the slow part, not the calculations. Each trade is read with fgets and sscanf, which come from the already-compiled C library, so the -O flags cannot speed them up. Our own VWAP, OHLC and SMA code only does a few simple calculations per trade, so at most it accounts for the small gain seen at -O2/-O3.
3. The variation between runs comes from the disk and the operating system, such as file caching, OneDrive sync and other background activity. A faster parser (Stage A+) should therefore help much more than changing compiler flags.
